HOW TO PLAY

ROLES

🕵️ INVESTIGATOR: Find clues, discuss, and vote out the THEFT.

👮 POLICE: Once per game, secretly investigate a player to learn if they are innocent or the THEFT.

🔍 DETECTIVE: Gets an extra powerful clue about the crime scene.

🥷 THEFT: You are the criminal. Lie, blend in, and survive the votes!

FLOW

1. Roles are assigned.
2. Crime is revealed with clues.
3. Discuss for 90 seconds.
4. Vote a suspect out.
5. Game ends when THEFT is caught or THEFT outnumbers innocent players.
